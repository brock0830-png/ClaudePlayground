## Independent check against TradingView bars

TradingView `CME_MINI:ES1!` 30-minute bars (pulled through the TradingView connector on 2026-09-24) match the Databento
period highs and lows tick for tick for periods A-M on all three days, and running this engine on the TradingView bars gives the
same IB, POC, VAH and VAL. The only difference is period N: TradingView's 16:00 bar runs to 16:30, past the 16:15 RTH close.
On 2026-09-21 that alone moves the POC from 7833.75 to 7827.25. To reproduce these numbers on a TradingView TPO chart, use a
custom session 09:30-16:15 America/New_York, 30-minute periods, row size 0.25 (1 tick), value area 70%.

TradingView's own TPO indicator may compute the value area with a different expansion rule (single rows rather than the
Appendix 1 pairs), so VAH/VAL can differ by a few ticks even with identical bars; IB and POC should match exactly.
