# Phase 6: add-on search on TALOS (post-holdout, exploratory)
Panel data/proxies_ext (1990 start). Design 1991-01-04..2015-12-31, confirmation 2016-01-04..2026-09-18 (pseudo-holdout: seen once before), full 1991-2026. Band 0.02, lag 2, costs x1. All cells in TRIAL_LEDGER.csv phase P6 (grid: reports/phase6/phase6_metrics.csv, frontier.csv, metals.csv, selection.json).

Candidate add-on: (C2) buy-the-dip inside the core: when QQQ is above its 200d SMA and today's close is the lowest of the last 10 closes, hold QQQ at min(1, 2 x vol-target size) of the sleeve for 5 days; (B) a third sleeve of 30% of capital in GLD when GLD > its 200d SMA, sized 10%/vol20 (capped at 100% of the sleeve), funded pro rata from the two TALOS sleeves. Five new parameters (dip_n, dip_hold, dip_mult, w_gold, gold_vt); gold_len shares the 200d length.
